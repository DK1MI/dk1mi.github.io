---
title: Did you know you can send SMS via M17?
date: 2025-08-01
---

Well you can! Took me far too long to figure out but now I am having a blast with this!

![](/img/m17-sms-01.jpg)

There are many possibilities to achieve this but the most convenient one is to use [KD0OSS’s fork of OpenRTX](https://github.com/kd0oss/OpenRTX) on a Connect Systems CS7000 M17 (PLUS), Tytera MD-380 or a [Baofeng DM-1701](/m17-dm1701-mod/). You can either [compile it yourself](https://openrtx.org/#/compiling) or download the firmware (for the DM-1701) [from my server](/files/openrtx_dm1701_wrap). Ths feature will eventually be incorporated into the original repository of the OpenRTX team.

Here you can see the received message from above in the [CC1200 M17 Hotspot Dashboard](/cc1200-m17-hotspot/):
