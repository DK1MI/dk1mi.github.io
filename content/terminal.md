title: A retrofuturistic Computer Terminal
date: 2026-06-05
---

I’ve always wanted to build a retro system to go alongside my regular PC, but used systems are getting more and more expensive.
Then I remembered that I still had a few Raspberry Pis and a 7-inch LCD lying around. A quick search online led me to this nice 3D model:
[Raspberry Pi Display-Ständer](https://makerworld.com/de/models/1331917-raspberry-pi-display-standfuss). This design immediately reminded me of those iconic TV sets from the 1960s
and also of various old computer systems. I downloaded it right away and printed it with white PLA filament. I then installed the Luckfox 7" LCD and a Raspberry Pi 4B and set up Debian Trixie.

The result was this pseudo-retro computer terminal, which I can use to explore gopher holes, read [SDF](/sdf)’s BBOARD, and use various other terminal-based applications:
