### Tech News {#tech-news}

- [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com) - social news website focusing on computer science and entrepreneurship, popular among tech professionals and startup founders.
- [Engineering Blogs](https://engineeringblogs.xyz/) - curated list of blogs by software engineers.
- [jimmyr.com Technology News Mashup](https://jimmyr.com/) - personalized aggregation of technology news and articles from various sources, tailored by the site’s creator.
- [TechURLs](https://techurls.com/) - daily roundup of popular and trending technology news links across the web.
- [Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/) - real-time technology news aggregator that highlights breaking stories and trending topics in the tech industry.
- [Bleeping Computer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/) - popular source for tech news, security advisories, and malware removal guides.
- [Slashdot.org](https://slashdot.org) - one of the oldest tech news communities, known for user-submitted stories and in-depth comment sections.
- [Daily Rotation](https://www.dailyrotation.com/) - minimalist tech news aggregator that collects top stories from hacker and developer communities.
- [Hackaday](https://hackaday.com/blog/) - platform dedicated to hardware hacking, DIY electronics, and maker culture.
- [lobste.rs](https://lobste.rs) - programming and technology-focused community with a self-moderating user base.
- [progscrape](https://progscrape.com/) - scraper-driven feed of programming-related content from blogs and tech websites.
- [MetaFilter](https://www.metafilter.com/) - long-running community-driven forum covering tech, culture, and general interest topics.
- [AllTop](https://alltop.com/) - news aggregator that compiles headlines from top websites across numerous categories, including technology.
- [tilde.news](https://tilde.news/) - small, community-run news site by members of the tildeverse.

### Retro Computing News {#retro-computing-news}

- [Two Stop Bits](https://twostopbits.com/) - hacker news style page focused on vintage computing, retro technology, and historical perspectives on software and hardware.
- [Indie Retro News](https://www.indieretronews.com/) - reviews and news on retro games and new releases for old systems.

### Small Web {#small-web}

- [Bear Blog Discover](https://bearblog.dev/discover/) - trending blog posts hosted on Bear Blog.
- [Kagi.com Smallweb](https://kagi.com/smallweb/) - curated collection of small, independent websites promoted by Kagi as part of the “Small Web” movement.
- [ooh.directory](https://ooh.directory/) - human-curated directory of small, personal websites and blogs, celebrating the independent web.
- [Marginalia Explore](https://marginalia-search.com/explore/random) - a search engine and discovery tool for small, personal websites, offering a random site explorer feature.

### Ham Radio News {#ham-radio-news}

- [Amateur Radio Daily](https://daily.hamweekly.com/) - daily amateur radio news site.
- [Zero Retries](https://www.zeroretries.org/) - weekly ham radio digest / newsletter.
